Our Story

Select Transport Partners was founded when a group of former colleagues and friends decided to create a brokerage with a fresh approach. Drawing on years of experience in logistics, operations, and technology, we’re thrilled to deliver superior service to the agent-based logistics market.

Headquartered in Matthews, NC, we specialize in delivering exceptional pricing and support to independent agents and their customers, driven by our proprietary AI-powered technology.

While tech plays a vital role in our operations, we never lose sight of the importance of personal relationships. Our leadership team is deeply involved in the day-to-day operations, working closely with agents, carriers, vendors, and our internal staff.
